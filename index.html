Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what customers ask most — so you don't have to WhatsApp us!

How do I book a ride?▾
Simply fill in the booking form above with your name, phone, pickup and drop-off location, then tap WhatsApp to Book. We confirm within 2 minutes.
How much does a ride cost?▾
KES 80 for up to 3km, KES 150 for 3-10km, and KES 300+ for over 10km. You can also use our fare estimator to get an exact quote before booking.
How do I pay?▾
You can pay via M-Pesa Till 5440462 (Buy Goods) or cash on arrival. No card needed.
How long does a rider take to arrive?▾
On average 3-5 minutes within Eldoret town. During busy hours like mornings and evenings it may take up to 10 minutes.
Is there a helmet for passengers?▾
Yes! Every RidaSwift rider carries a passenger helmet. Your safety is our top priority.
What areas do you cover?▾
We serve Eldoret City, Elgonview, Pioneer, Huruma, Kimumu, Kamukunji, Kipkaren, Sukunanga, Langas, Kokwas, West Indies, Subaru, Kapsoya, Annex, Shauri, Mwanzo, Kahoya, Miti Moja, Roady, Tairi Mbili, Maili Nne.
Can I book in advance?▾
Yes! Select Schedule for later today or Schedule for tomorrow in the booking form. We'll confirm your rider in advance.
What if I forget something on the boda?▾
Use our Lost and Found section below — fill in your details and we will contact your rider immediately to help recover your item.
