Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers so you don't have to WhatsApp us for every question!

How does Swift Shopper work?▾
Fill in the order form with the shop name, items you need and your delivery address. Tap WhatsApp to send your order. We assign a shopper, they buy your items and deliver to your door.
How much does delivery cost?▾
KES 80 for short distances up to 3km, KES 150 for 3–10km, and KES 300+ for over 10km. You also pay the actual shop price for your items — no markup on products.
Do I pay for the items upfront?▾
You pay for the items plus the delivery fee via M-Pesa before or on delivery. We send you a photo of all items before buying so you can confirm everything is correct first.
How long does delivery take?▾
Most orders are delivered within 45–90 minutes depending on your distance from the shop and how busy the store is. We will update you on WhatsApp throughout.
What if the wrong item is bought?▾
If we buy the wrong item, we go back to the shop and exchange it at no extra charge. We always send photos before purchasing to minimise mistakes.
Which areas do you deliver to?▾
We deliver across Eldoret City, Elgonview, Pioneer, Kipkaren, Huruma, Kamukunji, Kimumu and all surrounding areas.
Can I order from any shop?▾
Yes! Any shop in Eldoret — Naivas, Chandarana, local pharmacies, hardware stores, electronics shops and more. Just tell us the shop name and we will go there for you.
How do I pay?▾
M-Pesa to 0716 651 788 or cash on delivery. Both accepted — no card needed.
